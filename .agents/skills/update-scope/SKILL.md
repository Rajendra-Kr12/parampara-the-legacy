# Update the living scope

1. Read the relevant feature section in `docs/scope.md`.
2. Record only facts supported by the conversation, repository, or checks actually run.
3. Mark checklist items complete only when the work is done and evidence exists.
4. Separate automated checks, manual checks, and checks not run.
5. Record changed decisions and why the previous plan was amended.
6. Keep blockers, risks, and next actions explicit.
7. Do not rewrite history to make progress look cleaner than it was.
8. Ensure `docs/decision-log.md` is updated for durable decisions.
